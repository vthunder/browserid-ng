Or verify in one call

Know who signed in — and who's answerable.

No adapter for your language? It's one HTTP call to /verify-access, pinned to your audience. When a human signs in, grantee === email. When an agent acted, you get both names and the exact scopes their human signed for your site.

@browserid-ng/verify → rp-quickstart example →
 signin.jsserver
// One call — a verified identity for whoever signs in
const who = await verify(assertion, audience, { allowAgent: true })

who.email    // "alice@gmail.com" — attributed to: the human
who.grantee  // "alice+researcher@gmail.com" — the agent
who.scopes   // ["post"] — what Alice signed for your site
who.issuer   // "gmail.com" — verified offline against DNS

// humans sign in the same way — then grantee === email.
✓

No company sits between you and your users. The assertion verifies offline against the DNS a domain already controls — you never call our servers at sign-in time, there are no client secrets to leak, and nothing breaks if we disappear.
