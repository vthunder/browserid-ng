browserid.me

A BrowserID identity broker — it custodies your signing keys and brokers logins for relying-party apps. There's nothing to do here directly; apps invoke the login dialog and signer for you.

Sign out of browserid

Ends your broker session and clears this browser's cached certificates. After this the login dialog will show no identities until you sign in again. (Your accounts aren't deleted — they reappear when you next authenticate.)

Clear cached certificates only

Drops just this browser's cached certificates and per-site grants (the emails and siteInfo localStorage stores) — without ending your session, so the dialog still lists your account's identities. Use this if a sign-in is reusing a stale cert.

Agent keys

Authorize a program to act with one of your identities — headless sign-in for CI jobs, agents, and services — without sharing a password or a browser session. Create and revoke agent keys here.
