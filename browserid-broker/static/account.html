        Devices & holders

        Each thing that acts for you — a browser, an agent, a service — is a holder, grouped into namespaces you organize. trusted holds a config certificate (can authorize new sites); login-only can reuse existing logins but not authorize new ones. Click a holder for its warrants. Revoking a holder logs it out and kills the access certificates it minted.
