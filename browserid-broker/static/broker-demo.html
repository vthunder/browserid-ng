Broker Demo RP

A relying party on the device-cert model. Sign in opens the browserid dialog, which routes your email to its identity provider (your domain's own IdP, or browserid.me's SMTP fallback for no-primary domains), obtains device certs, mints a fresh-key access cert, and returns the 4-object presentation access_cert~assertion~warrant~config_cert. This page then verifies it via POST /verify.
