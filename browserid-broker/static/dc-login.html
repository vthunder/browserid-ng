Device-cert login (DC model)

Two IdP-signed certs + a warrant: device→access (authentication), config→warrant (authorization).

This page runs the real device-cert login in your browser against this broker (IdP + verifier): a device cert + config cert, a fresh-key access cert minted through the mint API, a warrant signed by the config cert, presented as access cert + assertion + warrant + config cert and verified with real primary/fallback conformance.
