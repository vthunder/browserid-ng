Agent keys

An agent key lets a program act with one of your identities — signing in to apps and services on your behalf — without a browser. You authorize it here by signing a short delegation with your identity; the program holds a private key, and browser­id vouches for each use under your policy. Revoke any time; agents stop working within a day.
