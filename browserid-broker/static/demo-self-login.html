The browser is your first agent

A live demo of Model A: human login as a subject:self mint through the agent endpoint.

This page runs the whole login loop in your browser, against this broker: it self-signs a provisioning credential (subjects:[self]), then mints a login certificate for you through /provision/mint — the very endpoint autonomous agents use. No hidden iframe, no postMessage cert relay. The browser is acting as your first agent.
